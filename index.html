About me

I’m Aldane Hutchinson, a full stack engineer from Kingston, Jamaica. I’ve been programming for three years, and I like owning a feature from the first idea to a working release.

My strongest backend work is in Go and Python. I built Flaxon, an async Python framework with a plugin ecosystem, and Flaxon++, a Go sidecar for it. On the frontend I work with React, Next.js, TypeScript and Three.js.

I’m also CompTIA Security+ certified and I study Computer Networking and Security at UTech, so I think about authentication, validation and hardening while I build, not afterwards.

Outside of code: chess, open source, and following the markets.
